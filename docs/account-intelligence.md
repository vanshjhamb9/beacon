# Account Intelligence Platform (AIP v1)

**Version:** `aip-v1`

## Pipeline position

GOAP → **AIP** → Revenue Hunter → Sales Intelligence → Campaign Intelligence → Communication Gateway → Founder OS

## Purpose

Master enrichment: transform every discovered opportunity into a complete, evidence-backed, sales-ready account. Never fabricate emails, phones, revenue, employees, or decision makers.

## Guarantees

Every field includes confidence, source attribution, and last verified timestamp. Licensed providers (Apollo, PDL, ZoomInfo, Clearbit, Crunchbase) remain disabled until credentials exist.
