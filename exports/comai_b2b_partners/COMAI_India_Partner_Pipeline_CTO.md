## SCORING METHODOLOGY

Each lead is scored on 4 axes (0-100 each):

1. **CLIENT_ACCESS_SCORE** — Evidence of multiple business clients
   - 100: 100+ verified clients, enterprise logos
   - 75: 50+ clients, mid-market brands
   - 50: 20+ clients, SMB/startup focus
   - 25: 5-20 clients
   - 0: Unknown/few clients

2. **ECOMMERE_OVERLAP** — How much they work with ecommerce/D2C
   - 100: Pure D2C/ecommerce agency
   - 75: Strong ecommerce practice
   - 50: Mixed clients, some ecommerce
   - 25: Occasional ecommerce
   - 0: No ecommerce work

3. **RESELLER_PROOF** — Evidence of reselling/white-label/partner programs
   - 100: Active white-label/reseller program
   - 75: Partner program, referral revenue
   - 50: Cross-sells tools to clients
   - 25: May add services
   - 0: No evidence

4. **WHATSAPP_PROXIMITY** — How close they are to WhatsApp commerce
   - 100: Already sell WhatsApp API/automation
   - 75: Run WhatsApp campaigns for clients
   - 50: Use WhatsApp as marketing channel
   - 25: Aware of WhatsApp commerce
   - 0: No WhatsApp work

**COMAI_PARTNER_FIT** = (CLIENT_ACCESS * 0.3) + (ECOMMERE_OVERLAP * 0.25) + (RESELLER_PROOF * 0.25) + (WHATSAPP_PROXIMITY * 0.2)
